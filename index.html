Welcome to the Department

Established to impart quality technical education in the biological sciences, the Department of Life Sciences at Sri Venkateswara College integrates the comprehensive study of Botany, Zoology, and Chemistry. Our primary objective is to build strong conceptual understanding and practical laboratory skills to prepare students for advanced scientific research and industry roles.
